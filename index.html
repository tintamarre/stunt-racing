How to play

↑/W
Accelerate (in the air: lean forward)
↓/S
Brake / reverse (in the air: lean back)
←→/AD
Steer (in the air: spin)
Space/Shift
Arcade: hold while steering to drift, release for a mini-turbo (blue sparks, then orange for a super turbo). Classic: handbrake
E
Use your item (🚀 turbo, 🍌 banana, 🦘 spring, ⭐ star) — drive through the ? boxes to get one. Player 2: Right Ctrl
H
Horn (each car has its own!)
1–4
Math quiz answers (player 2: 7 8 9 0). First right answer drives off, the other is frozen 3 s
C
Change camera (chase, bumper, TV, heli, cinematic)
R
Put the car back on the track
G
Show / hide your best-lap ghost
Esc
Pause
2 players
Split screen. P1: WASD (or ZQSD), Space/Left Shift drift, H horn, C camera, R reset. P2: arrows, Right Shift// drift, . horn, , camera, Enter reset

New to the game? Pick an Easy track with Arcade driving: you can take the turns flat out. Turn on Auto return to track to be put back on the road when you leave it.

Hit a tree flat out and it snaps (TIMBER!), costing you a bit of speed; slow down and it is a wall. Races last 1 to 5 laps (set in the menu).

Race against up to 5 computer opponents (menu: Opponents). You start at the back of the grid — overtake them all!

Drive over every tile of the track, then cross the finish line. Blue boost pads push you forward — use them before loops! Gamepads and touch screens work too.

Click / drag to place · Right-click or R to rotate · +/− height (slopes climb one level) · E eraser · Shift+click to pick

How to play

↑/W
Accelerate (in the air: lean forward)
↓/S
Brake / reverse (in the air: lean back)
←→/AD
Steer (in the air: spin)
Space
Handbrake — drift!
C
Change camera (chase, bumper, TV, heli, cinematic)
R
Put the car back on the track
G
Show / hide your best-lap ghost
Esc
Pause

Drive over every tile of the track, then cross the finish line. Blue boost pads push you forward — use them before loops! Gamepads and touch screens work too.

Click / drag to place · Right-click or R to rotate · E eraser · Shift+click to pick
